## 3. Booking and paying

- **Prices and availability** are set by the facility and shown before you pay. Taxes are included or added as the facility has set them, and the total you'll pay is shown before you confirm.
- **A booking is confirmed** only when you see a confirmation (and, where payment is required, when payment succeeds). A slot may be held for a short time while you pay; if payment isn't completed in time, the hold is released.
- **Payment processing** is provided by **Stripe**. Your card details go directly to Stripe and are subject to Stripe's terms; we never see your full card number. By paying, you authorize the facility to charge your payment method for the amount shown.
- **Saved cards.** If you choose to save a card, you authorize the facility where you saved it to charge that card for bookings and extensions you request, balances you owe that facility, and anything else you've agreed to (for example, recurring bookings or a membership), in each case under that facility's policies. You can remove a saved card at any time in your account; removing it doesn't cancel amounts already owed.
- **Balances.** If you owe a facility money (for example, an unpaid booking or a no-show fee under its policy), the facility may request payment or charge a saved card as its policy allows.
- **Price or booking errors.** If a price or availability was obviously wrong because of a technical error, the facility may cancel the affected booking and refund what you paid for it.

## 5. Players, waivers and conduct

- **Adding players.** You can add other people to your booking. If you're adding a child, you confirm you're their parent or guardian or have that person's permission.
- **Waivers are between you and the facility.** A facility may require you (and your players) to accept its waiver or agreement before playing. Courtside records your acceptance for the facility but is not a party to the waiver and doesn't write or approve its contents. Read it before you accept — typing your name has the same effect as signing.

- **Facility rules.** Follow the facility's rules and staff instructions. The facility may refuse or cancel a booking, or suspend you, under its own policies.

- **you can always ask for a person** (the assistant will transfer you or take a message, depending on what the facility offers), and continuing the call means you agree to the recording and to the assistant acting on your requests;
- the assistant can make mistakes. **Your booking confirmation is what counts** — check it, and contact the facility if something is wrong.
