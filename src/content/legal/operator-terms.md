## 7. The AI phone assistant and other AI features

- **What it does.** The AI phone assistant answers calls to your line, answers questions using the information you configure, and — for callers whose phone number matches a customer record — can make, change or cancel bookings, charge a Customer's saved card with their spoken consent, send payment links, share door codes within the window you set, take messages and transfer calls, all according to your settings and booking rules.
- **Disclosure and recording.** The assistant tells callers that they're speaking with an automated assistant and that the call is recorded. **You must not configure it to hide that it's automated, or to stop disclosing recording**, and you must not use it for outbound calling or telemarketing unless we've made that feature available and you've met the requirements of the CRTC Unsolicited Telecommunications Rules, the TCPA and similar laws.

The assistant relies on the information you give it; keep your facility information, FAQs and settings accurate, and review call records. **Actions the assistant takes are subject to the same booking rules as any other booking, and you remain responsible for honouring confirmed bookings.** We'll work to correct errors you report, but we don't guarantee the assistant will be error-free.
- **Providers.** The AI features use third-party providers (currently Retell AI for call handling, OpenAI for the language model and ElevenLabs for the voice), listed in the DPA. **We don't use Customer Data, recordings or transcripts to train AI models.**
- **Your integrations.** If you ask us to forward call data to a tool of your own (for example, a workflow or CRM system), you are responsible for that tool and what it does with the data.
