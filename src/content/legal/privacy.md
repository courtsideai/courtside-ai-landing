# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 29, 2026**

This policy explains what personal information Courtside AI Inc. ("**Courtside**", "**we**", "**us**") collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have. We've written it to be read, not skimmed past — if anything is unclear, email us at **contact@court-side.ai**.

## 1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Courtside AI Inc. is a Canadian corporation located at 60 Spy Ct, Unit 2, Markham, Ontario L3R 5H6, Canada.

We make software that sports facilities (tennis, pickleball, badminton, basketball and similar venues) use to run their business: court scheduling, online booking, payments, memberships, waivers, messaging, door access codes, and an AI phone assistant. We provide it through:

- **book.court-side.ai** — the booking site where you book courts at a facility;
- **manage.court-side.ai** — the dashboard facilities and their staff use;
- **the Courtside mobile app** for iOS and Android;
- **the AI phone assistant** that answers a facility's phone line, where the facility has turned it on;
- **court-side.ai** — our website.

Together these are the "**Services**". This policy applies to everyone whose information passes through them:

| You are… | For example |
|---|---|
| **A customer** | You book a court, sign a waiver, buy a membership or pass, or are added as a player to someone's booking |
| **A caller** | You phone a facility that uses our AI phone assistant |
| **A facility operator or staff member** | You run or work at a facility that uses Courtside |
| **A visitor** | You browse our websites |

## 2. Two roles: when the facility is in charge, and when we are

Every facility that uses Courtside is an independent business. It's important to know who is responsible for your information:

- **The facility is responsible for the information it holds about you as its customer** — your bookings, payments, membership, waivers, notes staff keep, and the messages it sends you. We process that information **on the facility's behalf and on its instructions**, under a written agreement ([Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa)) that limits us to providing the Services. The facility's own privacy practices apply to it; questions about how a particular facility uses your information are best sent to that facility, and we will help it respond.
- **We are responsible for your Courtside account itself** — your sign-in, the basic profile that goes with it, your app settings, and account deletion — and for information about operators, staff and visitors that we collect for our own purposes (for example, to run and secure the Services and to support facilities).

### One account, many facilities

A single Courtside account works at every facility that uses Courtside. The **shared profile** on that account — your name, email, phone numbers, date of birth and addresses, if you've given them — is visible to **each facility you book with**, and staff at those facilities can update it (for example, to correct a phone number). Everything else is kept **separately for each facility**: your bookings, payments, waivers, notes and message history at one facility are not visible to another.

## 3. What we collect

### Information you give us

| Category | What it includes | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Account and contact** | Email address; first and last name; phone number(s) | Creating an account, booking, or being added to a booking |
| **Optional profile details** | Date of birth; home address; business name and address (for invoices); emergency contact name, phone and relationship; sport and time preferences | If you or the facility choose to add them |
| **Bookings and purchases** | Courts, times, players, add-ons, notes you add, memberships, passes, gift cards | When you book or buy |
| **Players** | Name, and optionally email and phone, of people you add to your booking | When you add them (see section 3.4) |
| **Waivers and agreements** | The name you type as your signature, the date and time, the version and text you agreed to, and the IP address and device/browser details at signing | When you sign a facility's waiver or agreement |
| **Payment details** | Card details are entered directly into **Stripe**, the payment processor — **we never see or store your full card number or security code**. We keep Stripe's reference IDs and, if you save a card, its brand and last four digits so you can recognize it | When you pay or save a card |
| **Communication preferences** | Whether you've agreed to receive promotional messages, and the record of that consent | When you give or withdraw consent |
| **Support messages** | What you write to us or to a facility through the Services | When you contact us |

**Operators and staff** also give us business details (facility name, address, tax settings, business contact information), staff names, emails, roles and phone numbers, an optional emergency contact, and a PIN (which we store only in scrambled, "hashed" form).

### Information collected when you call a facility's AI phone assistant

If a facility uses our AI phone assistant, **the call is recorded and transcribed**, and you'll be told at the start of the call that you are speaking with an automated assistant and that the call is recorded. We collect your **phone number**, the **recording**, a **transcript**, an automated **summary**, what you asked for (for example, "book a court"), the actions taken for you, and any message you leave for staff. Your phone number is matched against the facility's customer records so the assistant can help with **your own** bookings; it will only act on an account when the number you're calling from matches it. If you'd prefer not to talk to the assistant, you can ask to be transferred to staff or leave a message, where the facility offers it.

### Information collected automatically

- **Sign-in and security data** — when and how you sign in, IP address, and device/browser information, kept by our authentication provider and in our security logs.
- **Message delivery data** — whether our emails were delivered, bounced, opened or clicked, reported by our email provider, so facilities know a confirmation or door code reached you.
- **Door access events** — if a facility records check-ins or door access, when and where you checked in.
- **Mobile app** — if you turn on notifications, a push notification token for your device, its platform (iOS/Android) and the app version. If crash reporting is enabled, technical crash data that is not linked to your name or account.

**We do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or tracking pixels, and we do not track your location.** See section 9.

### 3.4 Information about other people

When you add a player to your booking, you give us that person's name and, if you choose, their email and phone so the facility can send them booking details and the door code. **Only add someone if they've agreed to it.** A player added with an email address becomes a Courtside account they can sign in to or delete at any time.

### Information from others

- **Facilities** may add or update your details (for example, at the front desk).
- **Stripe** tells us whether a payment succeeded, and the brand and last four digits of a saved card.

## 4. How we use it

We use personal information only for these purposes:

1. **To provide the Services** — create and secure your account; show availability; make, change and cancel bookings; process payments, refunds and invoices through Stripe; record waivers; deliver door codes; answer and act on phone calls through the AI assistant.
2. **To communicate with you about your bookings** — confirmations, reminders, changes, cancellations, receipts, payment requests, door codes and waitlist openings, by email and, if you allow them, push notifications (and text message where a facility enables it). These are **service messages**; you receive them because you made a booking, not because you signed up for marketing.
3. **To send promotional messages, only with your consent** — a facility may send you news or offers **only if you've agreed** or where Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) permits it, and every such message lets you unsubscribe.
4. **To keep the Services safe and working** — prevent fraud and abuse (for example, rate limits on sign-in attempts), investigate problems, keep security and audit logs, fix bugs.
5. **To help facilities run their business** — for example, reports on bookings and revenue, and indicators such as how recently a customer has visited.
6. **To meet legal obligations** — tax and accounting records, responding to lawful requests, and enforcing our terms.

**We do not sell your personal information. We do not rent it, trade it, or share it for advertising. We do not use your information, recordings or transcripts to train AI models.**

### Automated processing

The AI phone assistant takes actions you ask for (such as booking or cancelling a court) according to the facility's rules — it does not make decisions about you on its own initiative. Booking rules (such as maximum booking length or cancellation windows) are applied automatically, as set by the facility. If you think an automated action was wrong, contact the facility or us and **a person will review it**.

## 6. Where your information is stored

We are based in Canada, but **our service providers store and process information outside Canada, mainly in the United States.** While there, it may be accessible to courts, law enforcement and national security authorities of that country under its laws. We choose providers that protect information with safeguards comparable to Canadian standards and bind them by contract. For Québec residents: before your information is communicated outside Québec, we assess whether it will receive adequate protection, as Québec law requires.

## 7. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete or anonymize it:

| Information | How long |
|---|---|
| Your account and shared profile | Until you delete your account (see section 8), then removed after a 30-day grace period |
| Bookings, payments, invoices and receipts | 7 years, for tax and accounting law — after account deletion, these no longer show your name or contact details |
| Signed waivers (signer's name, what was agreed, when) | 7 years after signing, because they may be needed to respond to a legal claim. The IP address and device details are removed if you delete your account |
| Call recordings | 90 days |
| Call transcripts, summaries and call records | 12 months |
| Message history (what was sent to you and whether it arrived) | 24 months |
| Security and audit logs | 24 months |
| Consent records (when you agreed to or withdrew from promotional messages) | 3 years after the consent ends, as evidence under CASL |
| Push notification tokens | Until you sign out, turn off notifications, or 12 months of inactivity |
| Backups | Overwritten within 30 days |

A facility may ask us to keep its records for longer where the law requires it, or delete them sooner.

## 8. Your rights and choices

You have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you, and learn how it's been used and who it's been shared with;
- **Correct** information that's inaccurate or incomplete — you can edit most of it yourself in your account;
- **Withdraw consent** — for example, unsubscribe from promotional messages (use the link in any message or your account settings), turn off push notifications, or stop using the AI assistant by asking for a person;
- **Delete your account** (see below);
- **Receive your information in a portable format** (a structured, commonly used electronic format), where the law provides this right;
- **Ask us to stop disseminating** your information or de-index a link to it, where Québec law provides this right;
- **Complain** to a privacy regulator (see section 12).

**How to make a request:** email **contact@court-side.ai** from the address on your account, or write to our Privacy Officer at the address below. We may need to confirm your identity first. We respond **within 30 days**, and tell you if we need more time as the law permits. Requests are free. If your request is about information a facility holds as its customer records, we will pass it to that facility and help it respond.

### Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the mobile app (**Account → Delete account**) or by emailing us.

- **Upcoming bookings, an active membership or an active pass must be cancelled first**, so that no facility is left holding a court or a subscription for someone who no longer exists.
- If you owe a balance to a facility, we'll show it to you before you confirm; deleting your account doesn't cancel what you owe the facility.
- There is a **30-day grace period**: you're signed out everywhere, and signing back in during that time cancels the deletion.
- After 30 days, we remove your name, contact details, date of birth, addresses, emergency contact, notes, saved cards (including at Stripe), and your sign-in. We keep what the law or a legitimate need requires, as described in section 7: financial records (without your name or contact details), and signed waivers (signer's name, what was agreed and when).

## 9. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only what's **strictly necessary** to run the Services:

- **Sign-in cookies** that keep you signed in securely;
- **Local storage** in your browser that remembers small conveniences, such as which facility you last visited;
- **Secure storage on your phone** (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore) for your sign-in, app settings, and a copy of your door codes so they work without a signal.

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies or third-party trackers. If we ever want to, we'll ask for your consent first. Face ID and fingerprint unlock in the mobile app are handled entirely by your phone — **we never receive your biometric information.**

## 10. How we protect it

We protect personal information with safeguards appropriate to its sensitivity, including: encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our hosting providers; strict separation between facilities so one facility can't see another's customer records, tested by attempting to break it; access limited by staff role; hashed PINs; rate limits; verified webhooks from our payment and phone providers; audit logs of important actions; and payment card handling by Stripe, a certified PCI DSS Level 1 provider.

No system is perfectly secure. **If a breach of security creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we (or the facility, where it's responsible) will notify you and the relevant privacy regulators** as the law requires, and keep a record of every incident.

## 11. Children

The Services are meant for adults. You must be at least **16** to create an account; if you are under the age of majority where you live, you need a parent or guardian's permission. A parent or guardian may book for a child and add the child as a player — by doing so, they confirm they are the child's parent or guardian and consent on the child's behalf. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact us and we'll delete it.
